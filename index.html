House rules

Stop set before entry
  1. Rule I

    Process

    A plan before the open, a review after the close. Same routine, every day.

    Risk

    Risk is set before the trade, not during it. Protecting the account comes first.

    Getting better

    Journal every trade, find the mistakes, fix one thing at a time.
